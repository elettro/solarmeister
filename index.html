Signature mark detail

The three green bars above the M are a fixed part of the SolarMeister identity.

Three-line construction above the M

Use this enlarged detail as the visual consistency reference. Preserve the number of bars, their slant, spacing, proportions, and relationship to the M. Do not redraw, rotate, separate, compress, or reposition the mark.

  • Exactly three bars.
  • Keep the approved diagonal angle and parallel alignment.
  • Keep consistent gaps between all three bars.
  • Keep the third bar extending toward the M as shown.
  • Treat the bars and M as one locked identity element.

Brand basics

Fast reference for the Shopify build, approvals, and production handoff.

Core colors

Solar green #5DB13F
Ink slate #1A2530
Warm sand #FAF9F5
Clean white #FFFFFF

Usage rules

  • Use the official files without redrawing the logo.
  • Use black versions on light backgrounds.
  • Use white versions on dark backgrounds.
  • Do not move the three-panel mark away from the M.

Brand voice

German B2C solar ecommerce. Clear, helpful, practical. Lead with easy setup, savings, product fit, and trust. Avoid heavy technical language on storefront surfaces.
