SolarMeister social media

Social Media Assets

Ready-to-post images, carousels and videos for SolarMeister, built from the real shop catalog. German copy, product titles and highlights from the product pages, and every post links back to its product. New batches are stacked below as they are approved.

How to use

Image 4:5 for Instagram and Facebook feeds, Video 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Each card has a German caption and a download link.

Rules

No prices in the images. Facts only from the product page. Videos use songs from the SolarMeister music library.

Batch 1 · Product templates

5 products × 10 templates · 50 posts

Pilot batch to choose the templates that go on to the full catalog. Four stills, two carousels and four videos per product.
